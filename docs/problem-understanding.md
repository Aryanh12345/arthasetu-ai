# Problem Understanding

## Context

More than 190 million adults in India remain credit-invisible.

Traditional banking systems depend heavily on historical financial records, excluding:

- Students
- Gig workers
- Rural populations
- Informal workers
- First-time earners

Simultaneously, new investors face information asymmetry and complex financial products.

## Pain Points

### Credit Access

Users without formal history cannot access:

- Loans
- Credit cards
- Insurance products

despite demonstrating responsible digital financial behavior.

### Financial Literacy

Many individuals lack:

- Risk awareness
- Investment understanding
- Personalized guidance

### Trust Deficit

Existing AI systems often provide black-box decisions without clear explanations.

## Opportunity

Alternative digital behaviors can create:

- Transparent trust scores
- Educational investment guidance
- Financial inclusion at scale

ArthaSetu AI aims to transform digital responsibility into financial opportunity.
