# ArthaSetu AI

> Building Financial Identity Through Explainable AI

ArthaSetu AI is a transparent credit scoring and micro-investment platform designed for India's credit-invisible population.

The platform leverages alternative digital signals such as:

- Mobile recharge frequency
- Utility payment regularity
- E-commerce activity
- Savings habits

to generate an explainable trust score and provide educational investment recommendations.

## Problem Statement

Millions of Indians lack formal credit histories, preventing access to financial opportunities despite responsible financial behavior.

Traditional credit systems fail to recognize:

- Gig workers
- Students
- Rural entrepreneurs
- First-time earners
- Small business owners

ArthaSetu bridges this gap through AI-powered financial identity.

## Features

- Explainable Trust Score Engine
- Top-3 Feature Explanations
